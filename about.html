About Me

This game is a fully developed game by Rayan Aljoubory and was made for a university project. It is a simple yet engaging game that showcases the capabilities of modern web development.

The spooky platform adventure is built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the Phaser game engine. Explore three levels, collect lost souls, and save your score to the site's local-storage leaderboard.

If you would like to learn more about the development process or have any questions, feel free to contact me at my email: rayan.aljoubory12@gmail.com
